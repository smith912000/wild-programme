Questions

Before you step in.

Do I need any experience?

No. Most people start with the free Seven Nights or the Open Door. The courses begin at the beginning, and the weekly call is open to every level.

Are the courses extra?

No. Every WILD course is included in membership. The subscription is the only thing we sell.

What happens after I join?

You pay through Whop. Your Whop account gives you the members' side of the WILD Discord, and you use the same account to sign in to WILD OS.

What is the founding price?

The first 100 members pay £13 a month and keep that price for as long as they stay subscribed. Founding members also get the Founding Member role in the Discord.

Can I cancel?

Yes, any time, online, from your Whop account under your memberships. No calls or emails needed. You keep access until the end of the period you have paid for.

Is there a refund?

If you cancel within 30 days of your first payment, email smith@negotiorum.com for a full refund of that payment. After that, cancelling stops future charges.

Is this therapy or medical advice?

No. WILD is a contemplative and educational practice. If you have a sleep disorder or a mental health condition, speak to a professional before changing how you sleep.
