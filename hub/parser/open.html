The Symbolic Parser

A reading as a closed system.

Choose a suit and place three numbers into their containers. The parser reduces each one, finds what repeats, traces the movement between them and names the tension they hold. It does not predict. It reads structure.

The matrix

  • Height. Every figure sits at the height of its number, 0 at the bottom and 9 at the top. The line through the three large figures is the trajectory: rising means momentum building, falling means a return to source, a peak means a swell that falls back.
  • Columns. I is the seed, your single digit. II is the bridge, your two digits. III is the field, your three digits. The large figure in each column is its root, the single number its digits reduce to.
  • Dashed lines. The small figures are the separate digits inside a container. Dashed lines run from them into the root they add up to. That is the reaction: what two or three forces produce when they meet.
  • Gold. A number that repeats turns gold, and a dotted thread joins every place it appears. It is the echo, the centre of gravity the whole reading circles.
  • Dimmed figures. Numbers that appear nowhere in the reading are dimmed on the left edge. They are the void, the energies this reading does not contain.
  • The lower line. The polarity. On a scale of 0 to 9 it places the centre of gravity against the root that stands furthest from it. The wider the gap, the stronger the pull between them.
  • The faint symbol behind. Your suit. Its element sets the laws every number in the map moves under.

The same suit and numbers always draw the same map.
