What the Inner Room is held in

Three Pillars. Two Languages. One Room.

The Inner Room follows two axes that match the rest of the work. Three stages of cultivation — Body, Mind, Soul. Two languages — scientific, spiritual. Members enter wherever they are, and the room makes room for the movement.

Pillar
Scientific framing
Spiritual framing
⬡ Body
Sleep architecture, the inner clock, food, movement, the quiet allies, reading the body's signals.
The body as the instrument the soul plays. What the old practices knew about food, breath, posture, and the night.
◈ Mind
Nervous system, attention, the vagal channel, polyvagal practice, working memory.
Inner work, the figures you meet, what you have refused, the parts of yourself the night returns.
✦ Soul
The neuroscience of awareness, the third state between sleep and waking, time on the other side.
The crossing, the long sequences, the deeper waters, the place every tradition named differently.

⬡ The quiet rule of the Inner Room. Both languages are welcome. Translation between them is part of the practice. No one has to choose a side; the work is older than the split.

Honest limits

What The Inner Room Is Not.

Set the expectations honestly so you can decide. The room only works when the people inside know what they joined.

Not only a course

The beginner course is included, but the room is mostly what happens between the lessons.

Not therapy

If you need a therapist, please find a real one. Inner work is welcomed; clinical work is not the room's job.

Not a fan club

The room is held, not centred on one person. The practice is the centre, not the founder.

Not a chatroom

Quiet days are normal. The room is slower than ordinary servers. That is by design.

Not anonymous

You do not have to use your legal name, but you are a person here, not a handle. People remember each other.

Not forever, unless

You can leave any month. The room is for now, while now is when you need it.

Questions

Things People Ask Before They Step In.

Do I need to buy a course to join the Inner Room?
No. The Inner Room is open to anyone who has decided this is the work — whether you came in through the free Seven Nights, the open door, or somewhere else entirely. The beginner course comes with membership. Deeper courses are optional and sold separately.
What is the difference between the Open Door and the Inner Room?
The Open Door is the free side of the WILD Discord — anyone who has crossed any threshold can step in. It is faster, more open, less held. The Inner Room is the members' side: the weekly call, both tracks, the challenges and the course, held more carefully. Both rooms have their place.
What does "held" mean?
A held room has rules of conversation. People do not interrupt. Speed is not the point. The founder is present without being central. Questions are answered when they are ready to be answered, not the moment they are posted. It is closer to a study circle than to a server.
Can I leave?
Yes, any time, online, from your Whop account under your memberships. No calls or emails needed. You keep access until the end of the period you have paid for. Come back later if you want; nothing is lost.
What is a founding member?
The first 100 members pay £13 a month and keep that price for as long as they stay subscribed. Founding members also get the Founding Member role in the Discord.
Will my testimonial be used publicly?
Only if you offer it and explicitly agree. The room is held, not harvested.
Is there a refund if I subscribe and the room is not for me?
If it is not for you, cancel before the next billing date and nothing more is charged. Partial periods are not refunded; you keep access until the end of the period you paid for.
