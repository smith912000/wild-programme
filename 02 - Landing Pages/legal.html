1. Privacy Policy

This privacy notice explains how WILD (a trading name of Negotium Maximus Ltd, company no. 16548314, registered office 165 Main Street, Yaxley, Peterborough, England, PE7 3LD, the data controller) collects, uses and protects your personal data under the UK General Data Protection Regulation (UK GDPR) and the Data Protection Act 2018. Contact: smith@negotiorum.com.

What data we collect

  • Email address — when you sign up for the free 7-Night WILD Protocol or purchase a course.
  • First name — optionally provided at sign-up, used to personalise emails.
  • Purchase information — transaction records from PayPal or Whop, including the product purchased and payment amount. We do not store payment card details.
  • No tracking — we do not run analytics, session recording or advertising scripts. Our fonts and scripts are served from our own site, so visiting a page does not send your IP address to Google or other third parties.

How we use your data

  • To send the free protocol PDF you requested.
  • To deliver course content and community access you have purchased.
  • To send follow-up educational emails related to lucid dreaming (you can unsubscribe at any time).
  • To respond to support enquiries.
  • To improve our products and marketing, only if you accept analytics in the banner (none run by default).

Legal basis for processing

  • Consent — for marketing emails. You gave consent when you submitted your email address. You can withdraw consent at any time by clicking "unsubscribe" in any email.
  • Contract — for processing purchases and delivering products you have bought.
  • Legitimate interests — for fraud prevention and basic business administration.

Third-party processors

We use the following third-party services to operate the business. Each has its own privacy policy.

  • Kit (kit.com) — email marketing and subscriber management. Kit Privacy Policy
  • PayPal — payment processing for one-time course purchases. PayPal Privacy Policy
  • Whop (whop.com) — subscription billing and community access management. Whop Privacy Policy
  • GitHub Pages — website hosting. GitHub Privacy Policy
  • Discord — the members' community. Discord Privacy Policy
  • n8n (n8n GmbH, hosted in the EU) — checks your membership when you sign in, and stores the WILD OS data you choose to sync (journal, practice logs, intake answers, settings) against your Whop user id so it follows you between devices. You can delete it from WILD OS settings or by emailing us.

International transfers. Kit, Whop, GitHub and Discord are based in the United States. Where a provider is certified under the UK Extension to the EU-US Data Privacy Framework we rely on that; otherwise we rely on the provider's data processing agreement with the UK International Data Transfer Addendum.

Local storage. Our sites use your browser's local storage only to keep you signed in, to remember your cookie choice, and to keep a record of the consents you gave at checkout. These are strictly necessary and set no tracking cookies. We run no analytics unless you accept them in the banner.

Your rights

Under UK GDPR you have the right to:

  • Access the personal data we hold about you.
  • Correct inaccurate data.
  • Request deletion of your data ("right to be forgotten").
  • Object to or restrict certain types of processing.
  • Data portability — receive your data in a machine-readable format.
  • Withdraw consent at any time (for email marketing — use the unsubscribe link in any email).

To exercise any right, email smith@negotiorum.com. We will respond within one month.

Complaints. If you are unhappy with how we handle your data or your marketing choices, email us and we will acknowledge within 30 days and reply with what we have done. You can also complain to the Information Commissioner's Office at ico.org.uk/make-a-complaint.

Data retention

We retain your email address and name for as long as you remain subscribed. If you unsubscribe and have no active purchase, your data is deleted within 90 days. Purchase records are retained for 7 years as required by UK tax law.

Cookies

This website sets no analytics or advertising cookies. If you sign in as a member, your sign-in session is kept in your browser's local storage so you stay signed in.

We do not use advertising cookies, retargeting pixels, or sell data to third parties.
